# DECISION-tau-provider-chatgpt-curated-vcr: Bound curated VCR evidence to successful streams

Authority: unconfirmed

The public curated VCR corpus is synthetic successful-stream compatibility
evidence. It is not authority for scheduling, attempts, retry, concurrency, prose,
or timing, and recorded delay does not affect functional replay.

The corpus does not grow an attempt-sequence language. A broader format requires a
separately confirmed decision and an explicitly versioned schema or specification,
rather than turning compatibility evidence into scheduler authority.

The corpus contract is specified by
[`SPEC-tau-provider-chatgpt-curated-vcr`](SPEC-tau-provider-chatgpt-curated-vcr.md).
