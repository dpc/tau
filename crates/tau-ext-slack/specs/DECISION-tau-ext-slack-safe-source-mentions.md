# DECISION-tau-ext-slack-safe-source-mentions: Verified source mentions

Authority: confirmed, 2026-07-15, dpc

A send may optionally mention only the verified human source bound to a live
`reply_to` selector. It cannot mention arbitrary identities or proactive
recipients, and presentation never grants routing or sender authority.

Exact eligibility, parsing, wire form, bounds, persistence, and errors are
[SPEC-tau-ext-slack-source-mentions](SPEC-tau-ext-slack-source-mentions.md).
